## 🎯 Business Questions It Answers

- Which cities and properties drive revenue, and which are lagging?
- Where is occupancy high but revenue low, pointing to a pricing gap?
- How do weekday and weekend performance differ?
- Which booking channels bring the most bookings, and how dependent are we on third parties?
- How much potential revenue is lost to cancellations?
- Which properties have a guest experience problem?

## 🔍 Key Insights

| Area | Insight |
|---|---|
| **City Performance** | Mumbai leads revenue at 668.6M. Delhi has the highest occupancy (60.5%) and rating (3.8) but the lowest revenue, so there is a pricing opportunity. |
| **Guest Experience** | Bangalore ranks second in revenue (420.4M) but has the lowest occupancy (55.8%) and rating (3.4), signalling an experience gap. |
| **Weekday vs Weekend** | Weekends run at 62.6% occupancy versus 56% on weekdays. Weekdays still bring in 1.18bn (about 69% of bookings) against 0.52bn on weekends, so midweek offers could lift demand. |
| **Booking Channels** | Third-party platforms like MakeMyTrip (20%) and LogTrip (11%) dominate, while direct channels are only around 15%. Growing direct bookings can cut commission costs. |
| **Room Class & Category** | Elite rooms take 37% of bookings. Luxury contributes 61.6% of revenue and Business 38.4%. |
| **Property Benchmarking** | Atliq Exotica leads with 320M in revenue, while Atliq Seasons trails at 66M with a 2.29 rating and needs a closer review. |
| **Cancellations & Realisation** | With 24.8% cancellations, reducing them is a direct lever for realised revenue. |

## 📐 Metric Definitions

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RevPAR** | Revenue per available room |
| **DSRN** | Daily sellable room nights |
| **ADR** | Average daily rate |
| **DBRN** | Daily booked room nights |
| **DURN** | Daily utilised room nights |
| **Realisation %** | Share of booking value actually realised after cancellations |
| **Occupancy %** | Utilised room nights as a share of sellable room nights |

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- **Power BI** for dashboard design and interactivity
- **DAX** for KPI measures (Revenue, Occupancy %, RevPAR, ADR, DSRN, Realisation %, Cancellation %)
